The artist

From Omagh, with a love for the road.

Andrew Dolan is an Irish singer-songwriter from Omagh, blending folk roots with a modern sound. Carrying his guitar from pubs to music halls, he is building a growing reputation for original songs, live shows and an honest connection with every room.
